Honda Vehicle

Honda Civic:
Base Price: $24,000 (Sedan LX) / $24,950 (Hatchback LX)
Trims: LX, Sport, EX, EX-L, Sport Touring, Si, Type R
Engine Options:
  - 2.0L 4-cylinder: 158 hp, 138 lb-ft torque (LX/Sport)
  - 1.5L turbocharged 4-cylinder: 192 hp, 192 lb-ft torque (EX and above)
  - Civic Si: 1.5L turbo, 200 hp, 6-speed manual only
  - Civic Type R: 2.0L VTEC Turbo, 315 hp, 6-speed manual, 0–60 in 5.4 sec
Transmission: CVT standard; 6-speed manual on Si and Type R
Fuel Economy: Up to 36 mpg city / 46 mpg highway (1.5T CVT)
Safety: Honda Sensing suite standard on all trims (CMBS, LKAS, ACC, RDM)
Infotainment: 7-inch or 9-inch Honda CONNECT touchscreen, Apple CarPlay / Android Auto wireless
Colors: Sonic Gray Pearl, Aegean Blue, Rallye Red, Lunar Silver, Crystal Black, Boost Blue (Type R)
Warranty: 3-year/36,000-mile basic, 5-year/60,000-mile powertrain

Honda Financing & Service:
Honda Financial Services: Financing from 0.9% APR (well-qualified buyers), lease options available
Honda Care (Extended Warranty): Available up to 8 years / 120,000 miles
Maintenance Minder: Oil life monitoring system, standard on all models
Roadside Assistance: 3-year / 36,000-mile roadside assistance included with new vehicle purchase

Performance Trims:
Civic Si: sedan only, 1.5L turbo, 200 hp and 192 lb-ft, 6-speed manual only
Civic Type R: hatchback only, 2.0L turbo, 315 hp and 310 lb-ft, 6-speed manual only
Civic LX: 2.0L 4-cylinder, 158 hp and 138 lb-ft, CVT

Common Questions:
Q: Which Civic trims come with a manual transmission? A: The Si and the Type R, both 6-speed manual only.
Q: Does the Civic come as a hatchback? A: Yes, the LX is offered as a sedan or hatchback, and the Type R is a hatchback.
Q: Does every Civic have Honda Sensing? A: Yes, it is standard on all trims.
Q: How long is the powertrain warranty? A: 5 years or 60,000 miles.
