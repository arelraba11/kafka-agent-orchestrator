How It Differs from the Galaxy S25 Ultra:
The Galaxy S25 is the compact, standard model: a 6.2-inch display, a 4000mAh battery and an aluminum frame. It has no S Pen and no 200MP camera, which are found on the larger Galaxy S25 Ultra.
