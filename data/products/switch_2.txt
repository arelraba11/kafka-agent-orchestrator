Nintendo Switch 2

Nintendo Switch 2:
Price: $449.99 (console only, US launch)
Storage: 256GB (UFS)
Display: 7.9-inch wide color gamut LCD, 1920×1080, HDR10, variable refresh rate up to 120Hz
Processor: custom processor made by NVIDIA
Released: June 5, 2025

Display Output and Performance:
TV mode: up to 3840×2160 (4K) at 60fps, up to 120fps at 1080p or 1440p
Handheld and tabletop mode: up to 1920×1080
Wi-Fi: Wi-Fi 6

Battery and Controllers:
Battery: 5220mAh lithium-ion, about 2 to 6.5 hours depending on the game
Charging time: about 3 hours
Joy-Con 2: each has a mouse sensor, plus an accelerometer and gyroscope
Storage expansion: microSD Express cards only, up to 2TB

Weight:
Console: about 0.88 lb, about 1.18 lb with Joy-Con 2 attached

Bundles and Compatibility:
Nintendo Switch 2 and Mario Kart World Bundle at launch: $499.99
Both Nintendo Switch 2 and Nintendo Switch game cards can be inserted into the console.

Common Questions:
Q: How much storage does the Switch 2 have? A: 256GB of built-in storage, expandable with microSD Express cards up to 2TB.
Q: Does it play in 4K? A: Yes, on a TV in TV mode, up to 4K at 60fps.
Q: How long does the battery last? A: About 2 to 6.5 hours, depending on the game.
