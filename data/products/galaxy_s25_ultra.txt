How It Differs from the Galaxy S25:
The Galaxy S25 Ultra is the largest and most capable phone of the Galaxy S25 family: a 6.9-inch display, a 5000mAh battery, a 200MP main camera and a built-in S Pen. The standard Galaxy S25 is a smaller 6.2-inch phone without an S Pen.

Common Questions:
Q: Does the Galaxy S25 Ultra have a microSD slot? A: No, storage is fixed at 256GB, 512GB or 1TB.
Q: Does it support the S Pen? A: Yes, the S Pen is built in.
Q: How fast does it charge? A: 45W wired and 15W wireless.
Q: How many cameras are on the back? A: Four: 200MP wide, 50MP ultra wide, and 50MP 5x and 10MP 3x telephoto.
