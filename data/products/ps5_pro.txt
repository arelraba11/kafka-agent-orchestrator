PlayStation 5 Pro

Sony PlayStation 5 Pro:
Price: $699.99 (US launch, digital-only console)
Storage: 2TB SSD
Disc drive: not included; optional Disc Drive sold separately
Included: DualSense wireless controller, Astro's Playroom pre-installed
Announced: September 10, 2024, pre-orders from September 26, 2024
Released: November 7, 2024

Performance:
GPU: Sony quoted 67% more compute units than the PS5, 28% faster memory and up to 45% faster rendering
Upscaling: PlayStation Spectral Super Resolution (PSSR), AI-based
Display output: variable refresh rate (VRR), up to 8K output
Wi-Fi: Wi-Fi 7 where available

Add-ons and Comparison:
Disc Drive (sold separately): $79.99
Standard PS5 at the time of the PS5 Pro launch: $499.99, or $449.99 for the Digital Edition

Common Questions:
Q: Does the PS5 Pro have a disc drive? A: Not built in, the optional Disc Drive is sold separately.
Q: How much storage does it have? A: 2TB of SSD storage.
Q: What is PSSR? A: PlayStation Spectral Super Resolution, an AI-based upscaling feature.
Q: Does it support Wi-Fi 7? A: Yes, where a compatible router is available.
